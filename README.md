## 💡 Key Business Insights

* **Technology** generated the highest total sales among the main categories.
* **Phones** were the highest-performing sub-category.
* **West** recorded the highest total sales among the regions.
* **Consumer** was the largest customer segment by sales.
* **Standard Class** was the leading shipping mode based on sales.
* Sales increased significantly toward **2018** compared with previous years.
* The dashboard helps identify top-performing customers, products, categories, and regions.
